Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply for admission? +

Students can apply for admission through the official college admission portal.

What are the college working hours? +

The college department works from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

How can I contact the department? +

You can contact the department by phone, email, or by visiting the department office.

Where can I find the examination timetable? +

The examination timetable is available on the college notice board and student portal.

How can I check my attendance? +

Students can check their attendance through the student portal or by contacting their department.
